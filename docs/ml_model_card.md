# Model card: late-delivery risk (primary model hgb_time)

Generated by src/ml_final.py from reports/ml_test_results.json. Test split used once.

## Task
Predict, at purchase time, whether an order will arrive after its estimated delivery date.

## Data and split
Olist delivery-eligible orders (96,470). Train 2017-01 to 2018-02, validation 2018-03 to 2018-05 (threshold), test 2018-06 to 2018-08. Test: 18,603 orders, late rate 0.0548.

## Model
HistGradientBoostingClassifier on order features known at purchase plus time-aware rates (platform, state, seller) computed only from earlier deliveries. Plan fixed before the test run (see the header of src/ml_final.py).

## Limitations
- Late rates vary from month to month (1.36% to 21.36%); the model ranks orders but its average score does not follow the monthly level.
- The validation threshold was chosen on a period with a different late rate than the test period, so precision at that threshold shifts.
- One time split, one marketplace, about two years; no claim of generalisation.
- Feature importance describes association in this data, not cause.
- Delivered orders only; orders still undelivered at the end of the data are absent.
- Payment details are assumed known at purchase (no payment timestamps in the data).

## Findings (added after the single test run; the results above are unchanged)
- The plan was committed before the run (f9b8d57) and the test split was used once.
- The pre-declared primary model hgb_time did not generalise: test ROC-AUC 0.611, PR-AUC 0.0707 against a late rate of 0.0548 (1.29 times), versus validation PR-AUC 0.3020 against 0.1174 (2.57 times).
- logreg_base was better on test: ROC-AUC 0.687, PR-AUC 0.0947 (1.73 times), top-10% lift 1.74 versus 1.10. Bootstrap PR-AUC intervals [0.0873, 0.107] and [0.0658, 0.0775] do not overlap; they resample orders and ignore month clustering, so they are too narrow.
- At the validation-chosen threshold, logreg_base flagged 4,592 of 18,603 orders (24.7%) with precision 0.088, recall 0.398, F1 0.145 (validation F1 was 0.336).
- Within-month ranking of hgb_time: June 0.737, July 0.610, August 0.433 (worse than random).
- The cross-state rule, informative over the whole period (Module 4), scored ROC-AUC 0.371 in the test months. The cause was not investigated.
- The top-10% rule missed 907 late orders, 662 of them in SP.
- Conclusion: these models show modest ranking signal at best and are not reliable enough to support operational decisions. Validation results were optimistic because validation came from a different period. Choosing logreg_base after seeing the test results would use the test data for selection, so the pre-declared model remains the reported primary; a new untouched period would be needed to confirm any change.
